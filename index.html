Our story

Olive & Ember started as a single wood-fired grill at a weekend market. Ten years later, the fire's still the center of the kitchen -- every dish on the menu passes through it, from the vegetables to the whole roasted fish.

We work directly with three local farms for produce, and change the menu with the seasons rather than forcing ingredients into dishes that want to be made another time of year.
